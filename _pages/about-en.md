I started with a B.S. in computer engineering, then took an M.S. in life science and a Ph.D. in
molecular medicine. Since 2004 I have worked with genome, transcriptome, single-cell, and clinical
multi-omics data for over twenty years, and the analysis carries through to reports and services
that hospitals and the business actually use. Today I work at Cytogen as Chief Researcher and team
lead in the Diagnosis Division.

## Current work

- Built a model that assists cell image review, deployed it in house, and documented where it
  applies and where it does not.
- Designed, built, and now run four internal data tools together with the portal that ties them
  together.
- Preparing an oral presentation for GIW ISCB-Asia in November 2026, on a reliability map for
  per-gene multiome RNA velocity parameters in single-cell kinetics.

## What this blog covers

How the tools and pipelines I build actually came together, how far models and benchmarks hold
up once they are checked, what designing AI agent harnesses has taught me, and what I find when I
re-measure the numbers a paper reports. Most posts go up in both Korean and English.
